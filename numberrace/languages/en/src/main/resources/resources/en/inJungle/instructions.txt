Your journey starts in the jungle!  If you get to the finish before your enemies, you will be allowed to rescue a butterfly.

You will find two palm trees, each with coconuts. You should chose the one which has the most coconuts to go forward that number of squares.

Watch out, there are traps: sometimes your enemies will try to overtake you, or you will come across nasty plants on the squares. If you land on them, you have to go backwards.


Click on the flowers to choose your character...
