Your journey starts under the sea!  If you get to the finish before your enemies, you will be allowed to rescue a fish.

You will find two treasure chests, each with treasure inside. You should chose the chest which has the most gold pieces, to go forward that number of squares.

Watch out, there are traps: sometimes your enemies will try to overtake you, or you will come across nasty anemones living on the squares. If you land on them, they send you backwards.


Click on the shell to choose your character...
